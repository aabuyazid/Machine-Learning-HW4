\section{Math Question: Convolution Operation}
\label{sec:math}

\begin{question}
Perform convolution using the following matrix and filter, show your steps, and report your
results.

Input matrix (7 by 7), outer ring of zeros is zero-padding, interior 5 by 5 region is the active
data:
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 2 & 4 & 1 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 3 & 1 & 1 & 0 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 2 & 4 & 1 & 0 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 2 & 0 & 5 & 2 & 2 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1 & 3 & 2 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0
\end{pmatrix}
\]
Filter (3 by 3):
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 & -1 \\
1 & 0 & -1 \\
1 & 0 & -1
\end{pmatrix}
\]
Apply the convolution operation for all the following settings respectively and report your
results. Max pooling parameters (when used) are always Pool size: 3 by 3; Stride: 2.
\end{question}

\subsection{1.1 Without max pooling, convolution stride of 1}
\label{sec:q1-1}
\begin{question}
Calculate and report the dimensions of the output matrix and the complete output matrix. Show
your work.
\end{question}

\textbf{Output dimensions.} With input size $7\times7$, filter size $3\times3$, and stride $1$,
\[
O = \frac{N-F}{S}+1 = \frac{7-3}{1}+1 = 5.
\]
So the output is $5\times5$.

\textbf{Computation.} Since the middle column of the filter is all zeros, each output entry is
simply (sum of the left column of the $3\times3$ window) $-$ (sum of the right column of the
window):
\[
\text{out}(i,j) = \sum_{k=0}^{2} I(i+k,\,j) \;-\; \sum_{k=0}^{2} I(i+k,\,j+2),
\]
with $i,j = 0,\dots,4$ (0-indexed, top-left corner of the sliding window on the padded $7\times7$
input). Sliding the window across all 25 positions gives:
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
-3 & -2 &  2 &  4 &  1 \\
-7 & -1 &  6 &  4 &  1 \\
-5 &  0 &  3 &  3 &  2 \\
-5 & -5 &  1 &  5 &  4 \\
-1 & -6 & -3 &  5 &  4
\end{pmatrix}
\]

\subsection{1.2 Without max pooling, convolution stride of 2}
\label{sec:q1-2}
\begin{question}
Calculate and report the dimensions of the output matrix and the complete output matrix. Show
your work.
\end{question}

\textbf{Output dimensions.} With $N=7$, $F=3$, $S=2$,
\[
O = \left\lfloor\frac{N-F}{S}\right\rfloor+1 = \left\lfloor\frac{7-3}{2}\right\rfloor+1 = 3.
\]
So the output is $3\times3$.

\textbf{Computation.} This uses the same window values as in \ref{sec:q1-1}, but now only the
windows whose top-left corner is at $i,j \in \{0,2,4\}$ are kept (every other row/column of the
stride-1 result):
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
-3 &  2 &  1 \\
-5 &  3 &  2 \\
-1 & -3 &  4
\end{pmatrix}
\]

\subsection{1.3 With max pooling, convolution stride of 1}
\label{sec:q1-3}
\begin{question}
Calculate and report the dimensions of the output matrix and the complete output matrix. Show
your work.
\end{question}

\textbf{Convolution stage.} The stride-1 convolution output is the same $5\times5$ matrix computed
in \ref{sec:q1-1}:
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
-3 & -2 &  2 &  4 &  1 \\
-7 & -1 &  6 &  4 &  1 \\
-5 &  0 &  3 &  3 &  2 \\
-5 & -5 &  1 &  5 &  4 \\
-1 & -6 & -3 &  5 &  4
\end{pmatrix}
\]

\textbf{Pooling output dimensions.} With a $3\times3$ pool, stride $2$, over the $5\times5$
convolution output:
\[
O = \left\lfloor\frac{5-3}{2}\right\rfloor+1 = 2.
\]
So the pooled output is $2\times2$.

\textbf{Pooling computation.} The four $3\times3$ pooling windows (top-left corners at
$i,j\in\{0,2\}$) are:
\[
\begin{pmatrix}-3&-2&2\\-7&-1&6\\-5&0&3\end{pmatrix}\!\to\!\max=6,\qquad
\begin{pmatrix}2&4&1\\6&4&1\\3&3&2\end{pmatrix}\!\to\!\max=6,
\]
\[
\begin{pmatrix}-5&0&3\\-5&-5&1\\-1&-6&-3\end{pmatrix}\!\to\!\max=3,\qquad
\begin{pmatrix}3&3&2\\1&5&4\\-3&5&4\end{pmatrix}\!\to\!\max=5.
\]
Giving the final $2\times2$ result:
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
6 & 6 \\
3 & 5
\end{pmatrix}
\]
